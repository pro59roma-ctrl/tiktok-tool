LANAYA

LANAYA is a personal video-preparation tool. It helps its owner prepare short vertical videos from material the owner has the rights to use, add subtitles and an overlay banner, and upload the finished video to the owner's own TikTok account (as a draft in the TikTok inbox) and to the owner's own YouTube channel.

The tool runs on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other people.
