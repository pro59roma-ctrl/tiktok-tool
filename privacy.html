Privacy Policy

Effective date: 02.11.2000

LANAYA ("the Tool") is operated by MARTIN ("the Owner") for the Owner's personal use. This policy explains what data the Tool handles when it connects to TikTok.

What data the Tool handles

  • TikTok authorization data of the Owner's own account: an access token, a refresh token and the account's open ID, obtained through TikTok Login Kit. The scopes requested are used only to upload videos to the Owner's own account.
  • Video files that the Owner prepares on the Owner's own computer and uploads to the Owner's own TikTok account.

Where the data is stored

The authorization data and the video files are stored only on the Owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the Tool and are not shared with any third party, other than being sent to TikTok to perform the upload that the Owner requested.

What the Tool does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal data of other TikTok users.
  • It does not use cookies, advertising or analytics on this website.
  • It does not sell or share data.

Retention and deletion

The Owner can revoke the Tool's access at any time in the TikTok app settings (connected apps and permissions) and can delete the locally stored authorization file at any time. After that the Tool can no longer access the TikTok account.
